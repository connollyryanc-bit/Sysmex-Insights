Research Context

The new reality of
HCP information behaviour

European healthcare professionals are not passive consumers of information. They are experts who interrogate sources, cross-reference claims, and increasingly use AI as a sparring partner — not a shortcut. Understanding this shift is critical to designing an experience that earns their trust.

🔬

Europe's Clinical Culture

European HCPs operate in a high-trust, evidence-first culture. National regulatory bodies (BÄK, HAS, NHS NICE, IGJ, AEMPS and others across EMEA), IVDR compliance, and peer-reviewed data shape how clinicians evaluate products. Generic marketing language is an instant credibility killer. Depth is the currency of trust.

🤖

The AI Interrogation Shift

Since 2023, adoption of LLMs among European physicians has accelerated sharply across all EMEA markets. Unlike consumers, HCPs use ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity to pre-research, challenge sales claims, and cross-check clinical evidence — not to replace thinking, but to sharpen it before engaging with a vendor.

📡

GEO & Structured Expertise

Generative engine optimisation (GEO) is the new frontier. LLMs surface content based on structured expertise, semantic relevance, and E-E-A-T signals. A vendor website that reads like a product catalogue won't appear in AI-synthesised answers. Only genuine knowledge environments will.

How European HCPs "spar" with LLMs

Qualitative research and clinical specialist interviews consistently reveal a distinct pattern across Europe: HCPs treat LLMs as intelligent adversaries to stress-test their own clinical logic. They ask deliberately challenging questions, probe for contradictions, and use AI-generated answers as a benchmark against which they evaluate vendor claims. This is not naive AI trust — it is expert-level adversarial querying. The implication for Sysmex is profound: if the website's content cannot be surfaced and synthesised coherently by an LLM to match what a physician already knows, credibility is lost before the first click.
